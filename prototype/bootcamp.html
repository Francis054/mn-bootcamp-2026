How training works

Practical first. Career-ready by design.

The Foundation’s IT professional training combines technical practice with the skills that help graduates get hired.

  1. Hands-on practice

    Learning by doing, in labs and on real tools rather than slides alone.

  2. Soft skills

    Communication, teamwork and presenting findings, all part of the programme.

  3. Career coaching

    Support to turn new skills into job applications and interviews.

  4. A real-life project

    Each bootcamp includes a practical project applied to a real problem.

  5. Certification exams

    Foundation bootcamps include EXIN certification exams. Exams for this cohort to be confirmed

Where it all started

The Foundation piloted its first cybersecurity bootcamp in 2020 with 14 trainees. The 2026 cohort builds on that pilot.

Good to know.

Does the bootcamp cost anything?
No. The 2026 Cyber Security Bootcamp is free.
Where does training happen?
In person at the Maxim Nyansa Foundation premises, Coral Avenue – Tantra Hills, Accra.
When does it run?
Training started on 5 October 2026 and ends in December 2026. The graduation date is still to be confirmed.
Can I still apply?
Applications were taken through maximnyansaapplications.com. This cohort has already started, so watch the Foundation’s channels for the next intake. Next intake copy to be confirmed
How long is the programme?
About three months. The Foundation’s pages describe bootcamp lengths of 12 to 18 weeks, so the exact length for 2026 is to be confirmed.
