Motion spec

Motion is used to show change and guide attention, never as decoration. It only animates transform, opacity and clip-path, and nothing runs constantly off-screen.

01

Purposeful

Every animation explains a change in state, place or progress.

02

Fast to respond

Interaction feedback stays under 300 ms. Only content entrances are slower.

03

Cheap to run

Only transform, opacity and clip-path. Ambient loops pause when off-screen.

04

Optional

With prefers-reduced-motion, content appears in its final state.
