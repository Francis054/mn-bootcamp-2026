Why this bootcamp

Maxim Nyansa means maximal wisdom. For ten years, the Foundation has turned that idea into careers in tech.

01 — Mission

Career perspectives through IT

“To create career perspectives for young people with the use of information technology.”
02 — The bootcamp

Free, practical, in person

Six areas of security practice, taught hands-on at the Foundation’s premises in Tantra Hills, Accra, from October to December 2026.

03 — The symbol

Climbing the right tree

The logo is an Adinkra symbol meaning “climbing the right tree”: whoever works for a good cause can expect the help of others.
