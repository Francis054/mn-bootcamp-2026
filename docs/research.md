## 2. Verified facts

### Identity
- **[V]** The official website is **https://maximnyansa.com/** (S1). It has a French version at /home-fr/.
- **[V]** It is "a foundation established in Ghana in **2016**". It raises funds and mobilizes volunteers to train underprivileged young Africans, and describes a bi-cultural European–African approach, "the best of two worlds" (S1, S2).
- **[V]** The name means **"maximal wisdom"**. The logo is "a classic Ghanaian **Adinkra symbol**" that "literally means 'climbing the right tree'", meaning "He who works for a good cause may expect the help of others" (S2). Its Dutch sister organisation is **Climbing The Right Tree** (S2, S5).
- **[V]** The first pilots ran in 2016, in a junior high school in Accra and with a group of 10 young IT graduates. The work has grown steadily since 2017 (S1).
- **[V]** Address: Coral Avenue – Tantra Hills, Accra, Ghana. Email: mninfo@maximnyansa.com (S1, S9). **[!]** The GPS address appears as both GW-0845-5255 and GW-0845-5256 on different parts of the site.
- **[V]** The organisation uses two names: "Maxim Nyansa **Foundation**" and "Maxim Nyansa **IT Solutions**". The IT Solutions name appears on the bootcamp slide, the contact page and the older logo (S1, S9).
- **[V]** The site lists branches in Sierra Leone, Burkina Faso and Nigeria (S1 nav).

### Mission, vision, values (S2)
- **[V] Mission:** "To create career perspectives for young people with the use of information technology." The tagline is "Creating Career Perspective for Young Africans".
- **[V] Vision:** "Investing in young Africans, empowering them with relevant IT skills to drive personal, market, and innovative growth."
- **[V] Values:** Empowerment, Family, Hope, Quality, Synergy, Practicality, Humility, Equality.

### Programs
- **[V] Learning Transformation / ICT labs for schools.** The Foundation helps schools set up IT labs, trains teachers, provides hardware (desktop labs, smartboards, projectors), software (LMS, open-source content, school administration software) and consultancy. It runs the Computer4Schools campaign (computer4schoolsghana.org) and the Skuuni school-management SaaS (S3, S5).
- **[V] Digital library / E-Library:** library.maximnyansa.com, which offers open-source resources (S3, S10).
- **[V] Hardware refurbishment:** "Donate your end of life cycle computers – We will refurbish them and give them a second life in a public school in West-Africa" (S6). The 2025 alumni list includes a "Computer Hardware & Refurbishment" specialization (S7). **[A]** No source uses the exact phrase "hardware refurbishment centre". Confirm the name with the client.
- **[V] IT Professional Training (bootcamps):** a practical program with soft skills, career coaching, a real-life project, and EXIN certification exams (Maxim Nyansa calls itself the West-African EXIN partner). Tracks: Software Development, Data Science, Hardware & Networking, **Cyber Security** (S4).
  - **[!]** Duration varies by page: "12–16 weeks intensive program" and "20 trainees every year" on the homepage (S1), "minimum of 18 weeks" on S4, and "14-week bootcamp" in the 2020 post (S8).
- **[V] Vocational IT training:** the 2025 alumni group specialized in "Computer Hardware & Refurbishment" (S7). **[A]** Whether the client means this when they say "vocational IT training" still needs confirming.

### Cybersecurity bootcamp
- **[V] History:** the 12 Jan 2020 post says 2017/2018 trainees asked for a cyber security track. It started as a pilot with one trainer from the Netherlands and ran as an intensive bootcamp at the Tantra Hills training center with 14 trainees. KPMG Netherlands and Norway (CSR) helped with course content and training. The bootcamp was described as 14 weeks, leading to CompTIA Security+ and CISSP exams (S8).
- **[V] Cyber Security Bootcamp 2026** (homepage slide S1, LinkedIn S12): "Applications are open!" It is free and in person and covers network security, ethical hacking, vulnerability assessment, digital forensics, security awareness and incident response. **Training starts 5 October 2026. Training ends December 2026.** Venue: Maxim Nyansa Foundation Premises. Cost: Free. Applications at maximnyansaapplications.com.
  - **[A]** As of 20 Oct 2026 the cohort is about two weeks in. Graduation and career outcomes for this cohort have not happened yet, so the design should use placeholders for them.
- **[V]** The homepage quotes trainers with the title "Trainer Cyber Security" (first names only). Their quotes appear in testimonials from earlier years (S1). **[A]** Whether they are involved in the 2026 cohort is unknown.

### Impact numbers (self-reported on S1/S7, repeated by GNBCC in S16)
- **[V]** Values taken from the homepage HTML counter targets: **128 school projects · 76,000+ students with access to ICT labs · 14 IT bootcamps · 99 trainers engaged · 173 trainees graduated · 98% trainees in jobs · 800+ teachers trained.** The GNBCC article (S16) repeats 128 / 76,000+ / 14 / 99 / 173 / 98% and calls the 98% a "reported" employment rate.
  - Use these as Foundation-wide figures only. They are **not** cybersecurity-bootcamp statistics.

## 4. Hackathon and anniversary notes

### 10th anniversary ("Maxim Nyansa @ 10", 2016–2026)
- **[V]** Theme: **"10 Years of Creating Career Perspectives — Shaping the Future Through AI, Innovation and Digital Skills"** (S13, S14). Hashtags: #MaximNyansaAt10 #10YearsOfImpact.
- **[V]** **Anniversary cocktail** with GNBCC on **Thu 8 Oct 2026, 5–8 PM**, at GNBCC, Cantonments, Accra. Climbing The Right Tree partners from the Netherlands attended (S16, S17).
- **[V]** **Grand Durbar** on **Sat 10 Oct 2026, 11:00 AM**, at Maxim Nyansa Villa, Tantra Hills, Accra. The program covered reflections on the journey, **a cybersecurity panel discussion**, student performances, partner recognition and networking (S14).
- **[V]** The 13 Oct wrap-up post lists anniversary activities including "student activities, the **AI Robotics for Impact Challenge**, ICT lab activities, the cybersecurity panel discussion, performances, partner engagements, recognitions" (S15, read only as a search-index excerpt).
- **[V]** The GNBCC article reports the board chair mentioning a recently inaugurated ICT lab at Juapong R/C Basic School (Volta Region), done with the Queen Etornam BEit Foundation (S16).
- **[V]** Homepage banner: "This year, we are celebrating 10 Years of Impact" (a July matching-grant donation campaign) (S1).
- **[A]** The photos read "10th Anniversary 2016–2026", which matches the founding year on the site. Which event each photo shows (cocktail, Durbar or hackathon) needs confirming.

### Hackathon: possible link, not yet confirmed
- **[V]** On 9 Oct 2026 STEMAIDE posted on LinkedIn: **"HACKATHON NEWS: AI FOR IMPACT BY STEMAIDE AFRICA & MAXIM NYANSA IT SOLUTIONS"**, subtitled "Hackathon for Impact: They Came, They Coded, They Built" (S18). This confirms that Maxim Nyansa co-organised an "AI for Impact" hackathon with STEMAIDE around the anniversary. The post body is not publicly readable.
- **[A]** This is probably the same event as the "AI Robotics for Impact Challenge" in the anniversary wrap-up (S15), and the event in the photos ("Hack STEMAIDE, AI for Impact" banners, Google Accra AI Community Centre, dated 10/07, i.e. 7 Oct). I did not find a public source that confirms the venue or the 7 October date. A search-engine summary mentioned "five Accra schools" and a "Google AI Center", but no readable source backed this up, so treat both as **unverified**.
- **[!]** It is a different event from **Hack STEMAIDE 2025** (13 Nov 2025, Accra International Conference Centre, GDIW 2025, also themed "AI for Impact: Local Problems, Smart Solutions") (S19). Do not reuse that event's numbers (60+ participants, 8 teams, winners).
- **[A] Big caveat:** the STEMAIDE hackathon is an AI, robotics and STEM event, and the public material suggests the participants were school students. Nothing shows that it is the **cybersecurity bootcamp's** hackathon or that bootcamp trainees took part. Confirm before presenting it as a bootcamp milestone.

2. **Trainers and mentors:** names, roles, organisations and photos, and permission to feature them. (Trainers in the site testimonials are from earlier years.)
3. **Curriculum and milestones:** the official module list and order, the length (12 vs 14 vs 18 weeks; the site is inconsistent), certifications targeted this year (CompTIA Security+? EXIN?), and the graduation date in December.
4. **Hackathon:** Did bootcamp trainees take part in the STEMAIDE "AI for Impact" hackathon, or is there a separate cyber hackathon/CTF? Exact date (7 Oct?), venue (Google AI Community Centre, Accra?), teams, projects and outcomes we may publish.
5. **Anniversary content:** which photos belong to which event (8 Oct cocktail, 10 Oct Durbar, hackathon)? The panel speakers' names? Any official anniversary logo or lockup?
6. **Brand assets:** vector or high-resolution logo files, any brand guide, the official green (#88CC00 in the current logo vs #A9CF3E / #79A00A on the site), and whether a new deep forest green is acceptable as an extension.
7. **Naming:** "Maxim Nyansa Foundation" or "Maxim Nyansa IT Solutions" as the presenting brand for the bootcamp?
8. **Stats:** may we show the Foundation-wide counters (128 / 76,000+ / 14 / 99 / 173 / 98% / 800+)? They are self-reported, and the 98% is called a "reported" rate.
9. **Portfolios and career progression:** what trainee projects or portfolios exist? Career outcomes will not exist until after graduation, so the design should use placeholders.
10. **Founding year:** the site says 2016. LinkedIn's company-data listing says 2015, which is third-party and probably inaccurate. Confirm 2016 as the official year.
