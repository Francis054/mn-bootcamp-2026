# Media credits

The community mark and the boot animation are original. Neither is stock footage, and neither is traced from a third-party vector or clip.

- The mark is `frontend/src/components/hood-mark.svg`.
- The boot clip is drawn by `frontend/scripts/build-loader-console.py` and saved as `frontend/public/assets/boot/loader.mp4`, `loader.webm`, `loader-poster.jpg`, and `loader-start.jpg`.
- The fedora on the home page is a photograph supplied for this site. The untouched screenshot is `frontend/scripts/brand/white-fedora-source.jpg`. `frontend/scripts/build-fedora-gear.py` cuts the hat out and stitches the cohort wording onto it. The page uses `frontend/public/assets/gear/bootcamp-hat.webp`.
