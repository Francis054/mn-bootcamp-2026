# Media credits

The community logo, the boot sequence, and the gear photograph are original. None of them is stock footage, and none is traced from a third-party vector or clip.

- The logo and the gear photograph are the cohort fedora. The untouched screenshot is `frontend/scripts/brand/white-fedora-source.jpg`. `frontend/scripts/build-fedora-gear.py` cuts the hat out and stitches the cohort wording onto it. The gear section uses `frontend/public/assets/gear/bootcamp-hat.webp`. `frontend/scripts/build-fedora-logo.py` writes the header, favicon, and social files.
- The boot sequence is drawn by the page. It does not ship a video.
